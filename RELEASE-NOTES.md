# Portfolio v14 — 13 November 2026

Open index.html to view the portfolio. Keep the files and folders together.

## Changes
- Replaced the repeated decorative scenes with individual environmental, water, research, education and photography compositions.
- Restored an image-based footer, with a separate portrait composition for mobile.
- Experience artwork uses the owner's portrait as its reference. No generated stranger portraits are included.
- Retained the requested plain/graphic sections and limited selected backgrounds to their content or right-hand column.
- Soft image-edge fades and restrained scaling; complete CTA subjects, conference portraits, diagrams and contact paper plane.
- Photography uses the original 5798 × 3809 sunset photograph; numerical collection badges replaced with thematic labels.
- Thesis captions removed; a published Sangu basin map added with attribution and full-size preview.
